About

A practical engineering mindset, strengthened by data.

My work sits where battery hardware, energy operations and analytical decision-making meet.

I am a Ugandan battery and e-mobility practitioner, data scientist and renewable-energy researcher. As Managing Director and Co-founder of 369data, I help lead the development of locally relevant battery products, diagnostics, repair, repurposing, monitoring and technical training.

At Equatorial Power, I turn mini-grid customer, revenue and technical data into dashboards, automated reporting workflows and actionable operational insight across Uganda and the Democratic Republic of Congo. My earlier work spans e-mobility production and training, productive use of energy, clean-cooking programmes, MEAL, financial analysis and more than a decade of mathematics and physics education.
