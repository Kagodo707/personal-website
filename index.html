Professional Profile

I am a Ugandan data scientist, energy systems analyst and clean-technology practitioner working at the intersection of data, decentralised renewable energy and battery engineering. I serve as Managing Director and Co-founder of 369 Data Solutions and Consultancy Ltd and support Equatorial Power as a data analyst consultant for mini-grid operations and performance reporting.

My work covers mini-grid intelligence, lithium-ion battery diagnostics and repurposing, battery-management systems, e-mobility, productive use of energy, clean cooking, carbon markets, financial analysis and monitoring, evaluation, accountability and learning. I develop practical analytics systems that convert operational, customer and technical data into decisions.

I hold an MSc in Financial Engineering from WorldQuant University and a BSc in Physics and Mathematics with Education from Makerere University. I also completed postgraduate training in Data Science and Machine Learning through MIT's Institute for Data, Systems, and Society.
