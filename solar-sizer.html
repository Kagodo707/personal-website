Where do I find the watts?

Look for a sticker or plate on the back or bottom of the appliance, or on its charger. It shows a number followed by W.

If it only shows volts (V) and amps (A), multiply them: 230 V × 0.5 A = 115 W.

Fridges, freezers and air conditioners: enter 24 hours (or the hours they are switched on). The sizer already allows for the motor switching on and off.
